## Description

```
sOC AutoTask records what you do with the mouse and keyboard and repeats it whenever you want: the
same, faster or in a loop. Press Record, do the task once, press Stop and let AutoTask repeat it.

RECORD AND PLAY
• Records moves, clicks of every button, the wheel and keys, in any program.
• Global shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R to record and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P to play (configurable).
• Works with several monitors and scaled displays.
• Emergency stop with Pause, Scroll Lock or by holding Esc: it stops at once and releases every key.

SPEED AND REPEATS
• From 0.5× to 100× or any speed you choose.
• Once, a number of times or forever, with a pause between loops.
• Optional countdown before starting and time left on screen.

FILES AND EXE
• Save and open recordings, with recent files and drag and drop.
• Turn a recording into a small .exe that plays it on any PC, with nothing to install.

EDITOR
• See every event and tweak it: delete, trim the start or end, simplify mouse moves, change or
  insert waits, undo.

A small window that stays out of the way, always on top if you like and next to the clock when
minimized. No network, no account, no ads, no trackers. Free software under the MIT licence, in
Spanish and English, with light and dark mode.
```
